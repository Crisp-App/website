Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Crisp (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a habit tracking app for iPhone developed by Tessa Ballard. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Summary

Crisp is designed to store your habit data on your device first. The free version does not require an account. We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

When you use Crisp, the app stores locally on your iPhone:

  • Habit names, icons, colours, schedules, and categories (Premium)
  • Dates you mark habits complete
  • Reminder times you configure
  • App settings such as list/grid view and appearance
  • Premium subscription status (whether Premium is active)

This data is saved in the app’s private storage on your device unless you enable Premium iCloud sync.

Premium iCloud sync

If you subscribe to Premium and turn on iCloud sync, habit data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple’s Cloud Documents service. Apple processes this data under your Apple ID and Apple’s privacy policy. We do not operate our own servers for habit content.

Subscriptions

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives information needed to validate your subscription (such as an anonymous app user identifier and transaction-related data from Apple). See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Notification permission is optional and controlled in iOS Settings.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Premium widgets and the Apple Watch app read habit data shared between the iPhone app and its extensions via Apple’s App Group and WatchConnectivity. This data stays on your devices and in your iCloud container if sync is enabled.

Data export

Premium users can export habit data to a file on their device and share it through the system share sheet. You choose where that file goes.

Analytics and tracking

Crisp does not use third-party advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Children

Crisp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support.crisp@gmail.com
